## Personalization notes — internal only

- Send the financial version to corporate giving, community relations, local businesses, or alumni contacts. Send the equipment version to relevant hardware and software suppliers.
- Choose one concrete connection: a product that fits the vehicle, a local presence, an education program, or a technical focus shared with the team. Verify the connection before including it.
- For equipment requests, confirm the exact model, quantity, compatibility, and delivery needs with the technical team first.
- Keep the initial email to one primary request. Do not promise exposure numbers, hiring access, exclusivity, or benefits the team has not approved.
- Attach the packet after reviewing its proposed contribution levels and recognition. See `../research/editorial-notes.md` for factual sources and remaining details to confirm.
